Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas
  • Vanilla Greek yogurt (higher fat preferred)
  • Sliced strawberries
  • Dark chocolate (melted)
  • Chopped peanuts
  • Sprinkles
  • Parchment paper

Instructions

  1. Line two quarter sheet pans with parchment paper.
  2. Smush the 3 very ripe bananas between the lined pans.
  3. Spread a layer of vanilla Greek yogurt over the bananas.
  4. Add a layer of sliced strawberries on top.
  5. Freeze until solid (a few hours).
  6. Spread melted dark chocolate on top of the frozen bark.
  7. Immediately top with chopped peanuts and sprinkles.
  8. Return to the freezer to set (may need additional time depending on banana ripeness).
  9. Chop up and enjoy. Store in the freezer.
