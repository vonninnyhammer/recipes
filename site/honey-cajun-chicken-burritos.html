Honey Cajun Chicken Burritos 🍯🌶️

Ingredients

  • 500 Calories
  • 65g Protein
  • 42g Carbs
  • 8g Fat
  • 60oz Chicken Breast (1700g)
  • 2 Tbsps Garlic Salt (30g)
  • 3 Tbsps Cajun Seasoning (30g)
  • 1 Tbsp Italian Seasoning (6g)
  • 2 Tbsps Minced Garlic (30g)
  • 3 Tbsps Tomato Paste (45g)
  • 2 Tbsps Honey (40g)
  • 1 Cup Diced Onion (100g)
  • 1.5 Cup Diced Bell Peppers (200g)
  • 3.5 Cups Fat Free Mozzarella (400g)
  • Fresh Chives (10g)
  • 3 Cups Cooked White Rice (600g)
  • 10 Low Calorie Tortillas
  • 2.5 Cups Cottage Cheese (565g)
  • 0.5 Cup Shredded Parmesan (60g)
  • 2 Tbsps Cajun Seasoning (20g)
  • 0.5 Tbsp Garlic Salt (7g)
  • 0.5 Tbsp Italian Seasoning (3g)
  • 1 Tbsp Honey (20g)
  • 3 Tbsps Tomato Paste (45g)
  • 0.75 Cup Fat Free Milk (180g)

Instructions

  1. Wrap the burritos in aluminum foil, or put them in an airtight freezer bag
  2. Place them in the freezer and keep frozen for up to 3 month
  3. Take out a frozen burrito and loosely wrap it in a wet paper towel
  4. Microwave it on a plate for 5-6 minutes
