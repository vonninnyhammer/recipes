Instant Reese's Chia Pudding with 51g of protein!! 🍫😋💪

DessertSnack
Added 2026-09-22 Prep Unknown Cook Unknown Serves Unknown

Source →

Ingredients

  • Reese's Pieces (or any other nut-based chia pudding)
  • Chia seeds
  • Milk (dairy or non-dairy, as preferred)
  • Honey or maple syrup for sweetness (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, mix 2 tablespoons of chia seeds with 4 tablespoons of milk.
  2. Let it sit for about 5 minutes until the mixture thickens into pudding-like consistency.
  3. Add 1 tablespoon of honey or maple syrup to taste and stir well.
  4. Stir in Reese's Pieces (or any other nut-based chia pudding) evenly throughout the mixture.
  5. Serve immediately, as the pudding will continue to thicken over time.

Enjoy your Instant Reese's Chia Pudding with 51g of protein!! 🍫😋💪
