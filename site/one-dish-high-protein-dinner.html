ONE-DISH HIGH-PROTEIN DINNER! 🤯

Beef
Added 2026-10-12 Prep Unknown Cook 15-20 Serves Unknown

Source →

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) fully cooked and drained ground beef
  • 3 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 cup diced onions
  • 1.5 cups sugar-free marinara sauce
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Add the cooked and drained ground beef directly to a large rectangular glass baking dish.
  3. Add spinach, diced onions, marinara sauce, 4 eggs, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Mix everything together directly in the baking dish until evenly combined.
  5. Spread the mixture evenly.
  6. Top with 1 cup shredded mozzarella, covering the entire surface.
  7. Bake for 15–20 minutes, until the eggs are set and the mozzarella is melted, bubbling, and lightly golden around the edges.
