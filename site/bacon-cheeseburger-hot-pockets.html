Bacon Cheeseburger Hot Pockets 🍔

AppetizerSnack
Added 2026-09-24 Prep Unknown Cook Unknown Serves Unknown

Source →

Ingredients

  • Self Rising Flour (500g)
  • Greek Yogurt (0% Fat) (520g)
  • Garlic Salt (15g)
  • Italian Seasoning (8g)
  • Lean Ground Beef (96/4) (850g)
  • Diced White Onions (150g)
  • Pickles (25g)
  • Ketchup (15g)
  • Yellow Mustard (15g)
  • Light Mayo (30g)
  • Fat Free Cheddar Cheese (112g)
  • Center Cut Bacon Strips (Cooked) (75g)
  • Fat Free Mozzarella (280g)

Instructions

  1. Combine Self Rising Flour, Greek Yogurt, Garlic Salt, and Italian Seasoning in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Add Lean Ground Beef to the mixture and mix until well combined.
  3. Add diced white onions, pickles, ketchup, yellow mustard, light mayo, cheddar cheese, cooked bacon strips, and mozzarella into the beef mixture and stir thoroughly.
  4. Divide the mixture evenly among 10 hot pockets dough wrappers.
  5. Seal the hot pockets according to package instructions and place them in a freezer for up to one month before using.
  6. To reheat, take out a frozen hot pocket from the freezer, wrap it in a wet paper towel, and microwave on a plate for 3-4 minutes or toast in a pan or air fryer for a few minutes if desired.
