Kapalı Hamburger / Stuffed Burger 🍔

Beef
Added 2026-10-12 Prep Unknown Cook 25 Serves 9

Source →

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour (add more if needed)
  • 1 cup warm milk
  • ½ cup warm water
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 5 g instant yeast
  • 1 egg white (yolk for topping)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 kg ground beef
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Sauce
  • Pickles
  • Sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Mix the milk, water, sugar, and yeast, and let it sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the egg white, salt, and flour, then knead.
  3. Add the butter and knead again.
  4. Cover and let the dough rise for 1 hour.
  5. Caramelize the onions with olive oil, salt, and sugar.
  6. Mix the ground beef, salt, black pepper, and mustard, then shape into meatballs.
  7. Divide the dough into portions and roll them out.
  8. Add cheddar, meatballs, sauce, pickles, and caramelized onions to the center, then seal the dough.
  9. Brush with egg yolk and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  10. Bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes, until golden.
