## Ingredients
- [ ] 1.5 cups non fat plain greek yogurt
- [ ] 1 scoop french vanilla whey protein
- [ ] 1 tbsp raw honey
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] Optional toppings


## Instructions
1. Combine the greek yogurt, whey protein, honey, and vanilla extract.
2. Add your favorite toppings.
3. Let it freeze for an hour and a half minimum.
4. Enjoy!
