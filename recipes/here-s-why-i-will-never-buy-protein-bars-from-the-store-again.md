## Ingredients
- [ ] 1½ cups rolled oats
- [ ] ½ cup creamy peanut butter
- [ ] 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- [ ] 3 tbsp maple syrup
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] Pinch of sea salt
- [ ] 200g dark chocolate, melted
- [ ] Flaky sea salt for topping


## Instructions
1. Combine rolled oats, creamy peanut butter, unsweetened cocoa powder, maple syrup, vanilla extract, and a pinch of sea salt in a bowl.
2. Mix well until a dough forms.
3. Roll the mixture into 12 balls.
4. Dip each ball into the melted dark chocolate to coat.
5. Place on a parchment-lined tray and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
6. Refrigerate until the chocolate shell is set.
