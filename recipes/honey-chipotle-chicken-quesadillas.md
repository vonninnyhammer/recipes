## Ingredients
- [ ] 40oz Chicken Breast (1135g)
- [ ] 1 Tbsp Garlic Salt (15g)
- [ ] 1 Tbsp Black Pepper (7g)
- [ ] 7oz Canned Chipotle Peppers (200g)
- [ ] 1 Lime (Juiced)
- [ ] 2 Tbsps Honey (40g)
- [ ] 6 Cups Fat Free Mozzarella (670g)
- [ ] Fresh Chives (10g)
- [ ] 10 Low Calorie Tortillas


## Instructions
1. Season the chicken breast with garlic salt and black pepper.
2. In a blender, combine chipotle peppers, lime juice, honey, and cottage cheese to make a sauce.
3. Warm tortillas in a pan or air fryer until slightly crispy.
4. Place a spoonful of the chipotle sauce on one side of each tortilla, then top with shredded mozzarella and chives.
5. Fold the tortillas in half and cook for 1-2 minutes per side until golden brown and cheese is melted.
6. Serve hot as a quick and healthy snack or meal.
