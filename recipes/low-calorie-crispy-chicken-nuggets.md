## Ingredients
- [ ] 500g lean ground chicken mince
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1/2 tsp cayenne
- [ ] 1/2 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 1 tsp dry oregano
- [ ] 1/4 cup mozzarella cheese


## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, combine the ground chicken mince with the egg, onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, cayenne, baking soda, dry oregano, and mozzarella cheese.
2. Mix well until all ingredients are evenly distributed.
3. Shape the mixture into 30 nugget-sized balls or patties.
4. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and lightly spray with cooking spray.
5. Place the chicken nuggets in the skillet and cook for about 15-20 minutes, shaking halfway through to ensure even browning.
6. Adjust the temperature to 200°C (392°F) if using an air fryer.
7. Once cooked, remove from heat and serve.
