## Instructions
1. Mash the blueberries and mix in peanut butter.
2. Add shredded coconut to the mixture.
3. Scoop the mixture into large clusters.
4. Freeze the clusters for a few hours.
5. Melt dark chocolate.
6. Dip each cluster into melted chocolate.
7. Serve immediately or store in the refrigerator.
