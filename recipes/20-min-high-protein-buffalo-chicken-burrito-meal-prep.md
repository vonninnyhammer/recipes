## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 lbs shredded chicken breast 
- [ ] 1/2 cup buffalo sauce 
- [ ] 1/2 cup low fat cream cheese 
- [ ] 1 cup non fat greek yogurt 
- [ ] 1 cup low fat or non fat cheddar cheese 
- [ ] 8 low cal tortillas (I use OLE extreme wellness wraps)
- [ ] 1/2 cup cottage cheese, blended smooth
- [ ] 1/2 cup non fat greek yogurt
- [ ] 1/4 cup water for thinning
- [ ] 1 ranch seasoning packed


## Instructions
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the shredded chicken breast with buffalo sauce.
2. Add low fat cream cheese and mix well until fully incorporated.
3. Stir in non-fat greek yogurt and cheddar cheese to create a creamy base for the burritos.
4. Blend 1/2 cup of cottage cheese with 1/2 cup of non-fat greek yogurt, adding water as needed to achieve desired consistency for the high protein ranch sauce.
5. Spread a thin layer of the buffalo chicken mixture onto each tortilla.
6. Drizzle a small amount of the high protein ranch sauce over the top of the spread mixture on each tortilla.
7. Roll up the tortillas tightly and place them in an airtight container for meal prep.

Total per burrito: 
- 305 calories
- 43g protein
- 17g carbs
- 6g fat
