## Ingredients
- [ ] Dandelion petals
- [ ] Water
- [ ] Honey
- [ ] Gelatin


## Instructions
1. Gently infuse fresh dandelion petals in water.
2. Sweeten the infusion with honey.
3. Set the mixture with gelatin to create soft, chewy gummies.
