## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup cold infusion of marshmallow root (1 cup water poured over 2 tbsp dried marshmallow root or 1/3 fresh steeped overnight in the fridge)
- [ ] 4 tbsp gelatin
- [ ] 1 cup honey, maple syrup or sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup water
- [ ] 2 tsp vanilla or flavoring of choice


## Instructions
1. Add the marshmallow cold infusion to the bowl of an electric mixer. Sprinkle gelatin into that same bowl and let it bloom for 2-5 minutes.
2. In a small saucepan, add the sweetener and 1/2 cup water and bring to a rolling simmer until it reaches 240°F or until a light drop of that syrup into a separate cold cup of water produces a soft ball.
3. Drizzle the hot syrup into the mixing bowl with the whisk turned on low.
4. Once all syrup is in, turn the mixer to high and let it run for 5-8 minutes or until soft peaks form.
5. Add extract or flavorings in the last couple of minutes or once the bowl is cool to the touch.
6. Pour into a lined baking dish and let sit overnight to firm up.
7. Cut and enjoy!
