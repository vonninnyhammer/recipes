## Ingredients
- [ ] 1/4 cup hazelnuts, finely ground
- [ ] 1/4 cup unsalted butter, at room temperature
- [ ] 2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup (adjust to taste)
- [ ] Pinch of salt


## Instructions
1. In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine the hazelnut flour and softened butter.
2. Stir until smooth and well combined.
3. Reduce heat to low and add the honey or maple syrup, stirring continuously to prevent sticking.
4. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring frequently, until the mixture thickens slightly and coats the back of a spoon.
5. Remove from heat and let cool slightly before using as desired.

Note: This recipe makes approximately 3 tablespoons of Nutella.
