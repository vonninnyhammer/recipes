## Ingredients
- [ ] Apples (1 apple per batch)
- [ ] Water (1 cup)
- [ ] Butterfly pea flower powder (1 tbsp)
- [ ] Lemon juice (juice of 1/2 lemon)
- [ ] Date caramel sauce (for serving)


## Instructions
1. Combine 1 cup of water, 1 tbsp of butterfly pea flower powder, and the juice of 1/2 lemon in a container.
2. Ensure the apples are fully submerged in the liquid mixture.
3. Place the container in the fridge and let the apples soak overnight (or longer) to achieve a dark color.
4. Remove the apples from the liquid and use them as a garnish or enjoy with date caramel sauce.
