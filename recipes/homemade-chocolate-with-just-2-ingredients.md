## Ingredients
- [ ] 100 ml melted coconut oil
- [ ] 3 heaping tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder (about 24 g)
- [ ] Optional: 1 tbsp stevia or your preferred sweetener
- [ ] Optional: Small pinch of sea salt


## Instructions
1. Add the melted coconut oil to a mixing bowl.
2. Whisk in the cocoa powder until completely smooth and glossy.
3. If desired, whisk in the stevia (or your preferred sweetener) and the optional pinch of sea salt.
4. Cut a clean, empty milk carton in half lengthwise and use it as a simple mold.
5. Pour the chocolate mixture into the carton and gently tap it on the counter to remove any air bubbles.
6. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours, or until completely firm.
7. Peel away the carton, slice or break the chocolate into pieces, and enjoy.
