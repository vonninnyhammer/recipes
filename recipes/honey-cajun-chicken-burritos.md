## Instructions
1. Wrap the burritos in aluminum foil, or put them in an airtight freezer bag
2. Place them in the freezer and keep frozen for up to 3 month
3. Take out a frozen burrito and loosely wrap it in a wet paper towel
4. Microwave it on a plate for 5-6 minutes
