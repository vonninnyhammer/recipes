## Ingredients
- [ ] 3/4 cup almond flour (or any flour)
- [ ] 1/2 cup cottage cheese, blended
- [ ] 1/2 cup shredded cheddar
- [ ] 2 tbsp diced jalapeños
- [ ] 2 tbsp bacon crumbles


## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients and knead into a dough.
2. Form into 4-6 bagels. Top with a little more cheddar and bake at 350F for 25-30 mins.
3. Take them out, add cream cheese and enjoy!

Pro tip: When I make recipes like this, one way to improve the texture without adding a ton of extra carbs is to use about 20% regular or gluten free flour and 80% almond flour 👍 | Keto Snackz
