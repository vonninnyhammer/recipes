## Ingredients
- [ ] Blueberries — 125g (defrosted frozen blueberries used here, though fresh alternatives work equally well)
- [ ] Chia seeds — 30g
- [ ] Milk of choice — 90ml (unsweetened almond milk recommended)
- [ ] High-protein vanilla or Greek yoghurt — 160g
- [ ] Honey or maple syrup — 10g
- [ ] Optional additions: Juice and zest of 1/2 a lemon to elevate the brightness


## Instructions
1. Thoroughly combine all ingredients within a bowl or container (a 720ml/24oz bowl size is ideal) until the mixture is uniform and well-integrated.
2. Cover securely and refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours—or ideally overnight—allowing the chia seeds to expand, the liquid to thicken into a rich pudding consistency, and the vibrant fruit flavors to fully develop.
