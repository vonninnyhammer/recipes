## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 Large Pumpkin
- [ ] 1 Hen (Cornish for size)
- [ ] 2 Parsnips
- [ ] 1/4 Green Cabbage
- [ ] Shiitake Mushrooms
- [ ] 2 Large Carrots
- [ ] 1 Ear Corn
- [ ] Red Pearl Onions
- [ ] 1 Shallot
- [ ] 4 Cloves Garlic
- [ ] Fresh Herbs: Rosemary, Sage, Oregano, Thyme
- [ ] Salt, Onion Powder, Garlic Powder, Chicken Bullion, and Chili Powder to taste
- [ ] Lard


## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 360 degrees Fahrenheit.
2. Wash the pumpkin, cut the top into a lid, and clean it all out.
3. Add in the chopped vegetables, the hen, and herbs and spices.
4. Put the pumpkin top back on and smooth a layer of lard on the pumpkin skin.
5. Bake for 3-3.5 hours and enjoy.
