## Ingredients
- [ ] 10 slices centercut bacon
- [ ] 10 slices Canadian bacon
- [ ] 90g (6 Tbsp) maple syrup
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] Garlic powder
- [ ] Thyme
- [ ] 600g (~4.5 cups) shredded potatoes*
- [ ] Blended egg mixture: 450g (2.75 cups) egg whites, 4 eggs, 300g (1 cup) cottage cheese, 12g (1 Tbsp) corn starch
- [ ] 60g (2oz) cheddar cheese (topping)
- [ ] 10 burrito sized tortillas


## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 430°F (220°C).
2. Add bacon to a sheet pan, bake at 400°F for 20 minutes or until crispy.
3. Remove bacon and excess grease, layer over shredded hashbrowns. Bake at 400°F for another 20 minutes.
4. Crumble cooked bacon, combine with diced Canadian bacon, maple syrup, salt, garlic powder, and thyme. Layer this mixture on top of the cooked hashbrowns. Pour over blended egg/egg white/cottage cheese mixture. Bake at 400°F for 25-30 minutes or until firm.
5. Let sit for 10 minutes before slicing into 10 rectangles, add to a burrito-sized tortilla with any sauce or garnishes of choice, roll the burritos and enjoy!
