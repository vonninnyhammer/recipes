## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups fresh raspberries, mashed (plus more for topping)
- [ ] 1 cup Greek yogurt
- [ ] 1/2 cup milk
- [ ] 4 tbsp honey (or more, adjust to your sweetness preference)
- [ ] 1/4 cup chia seeds

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, mash the raspberries until mostly smooth.
2. Add the Greek yogurt, milk, honey, and chia seeds. Stir well until fully combined.
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours) until thick and pudding-like.
4. Stir before serving and top with extra raspberries if desired.
